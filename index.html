About

A project made with love.

NursePrep was built for my girlfriend — a nursing student preparing for the PNLE.

I know how overwhelming review can feel. Hundreds of topics, endless practice questions, and the pressure to perform. This project was created to make that journey a little less lonely and a lot more manageable — one quiz, one flashcard, one steady day at a time.

Every feature here started with the same question: "What would actually help her study better?" The answer was a calm, focused place to practice, track progress, and feel prepared — without the noise.
